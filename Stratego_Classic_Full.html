Classic US Ranks (Full 40 Pieces)

Lower number = higher rank (1 is highest, 9 is lowest).

Official 40-Piece Roster

  • 1 — Marshal (1x): Defeats 2–9 & Spy. Loses to Spy (if Spy attacks first) and Bombs.
  • 2 — General (1x): Defeats 3–9 & Spy. Loses to 1 and Bombs.
  • 3 — Colonel (2x): Defeats 4–9 & Spy. Loses to 1, 2, and Bombs.
  • 4 — Major (3x): Defeats 5–9 & Spy. Loses to 1–3, and Bombs.
  • 5 — Captain (4x): Defeats 6–9 & Spy.
  • 6 — Lieutenant (4x): Defeats 7–9 & Spy.
  • 7 — Sergeant (4x): Defeats 8, 9 & Spy.
  • 8 — Miner (5x): Defeats 9 & Spy. DEFUSES BOMBS!
  • 9 — Scout (8x): Can move any number of open orthogonal squares in a straight line!
  • S — Spy (1x): Defeats the Marshal (1) ONLY if the Spy attacks first! Loses to all pieces if attacked.
  • B — Bomb (6x): Immobile. Blows up any attacker except a Miner.
  • F — Flag (1x): Immobile. Capture the enemy Flag to WIN!

Movement & Board Rules

  • Lakes: Two 2×2 water areas in rows 4–5 (cols 2–3 and 6–7) cannot be entered or jumped over.
  • Equal Ranks: When pieces of the same rank attack, BOTH are eliminated.
  • Couch Pass & Play: Board auto-rotates 180° so the active player always commands from the bottom.
