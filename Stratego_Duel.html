Classic US Ranks & Rules

Lower numbers outrank higher numbers (1 is highest, 9 is lowest).

Rank Roster (12 Pieces)

  • 1 — Marshal (1): Beats 2–9 & Spy. Loses to Spy (if Spy attacks first) and Bombs.
  • 2 — General (1): Beats 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 & Spy. Loses to 1 and Bombs.
  • 5 — Captain (1): Beats 6, 7, 8, 9 & Spy.
  • 6 — Lieutenant (1): Beats 7, 8, 9 & Spy.
  • 7 — Sergeant (1): Beats 8, 9 & Spy.
  • 8 — Miner (2): Beats 9 & Spy. Defuses Bombs!
  • 9 — Scout (1): Moves any number of open squares in a straight line.
  • S — Spy (1): Defeats the Marshal (1) ONLY if the Spy attacks first! Loses to all pieces if attacked.
  • B — Bomb (2): Immobile. Blows up any attacker except a Miner.
  • F — Flag (1): Immobile. Capture the Flag to WIN instantly!

Key Rules

  • Equal Ranks: If two pieces of equal rank strike, BOTH are eliminated.
  • Lakes: The two 2×2 water areas in rows 3–4 cannot be entered or jumped over.
  • Turn Privacy: Pass the phone when the transition curtain appears. The board rotates automatically for the active player!
