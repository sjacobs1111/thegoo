      🧘 Mindfulness & DBT Power-Ups

      S

      Stop & Freeze

      Do not speak. Do not move. Freeze in place for 3 seconds. Let your hands relax at your side.

      T

      Take a Step Back

      Physically step one step backwards away from whatever triggered you. Give your nervous system breathing room.

      O

      Observe Body & Mind

      Check in: Are your hands clenched? Is your jaw tight? Is your breathing fast? Name what you feel: "I feel angry/frustrated right now."

      P

      Proceed Mindfully

      Ask yourself: "What action keeps my banked game time safe?" (e.g. Taking a 5-minute pause or drinking cold water).

      T - Temperature

      Ice Water Reset

      Splash your face with ice-cold water or hold a cold washcloth over your eyes/cheeks for 30 seconds to activate the dive reflex.

      I - Intense Exercise

      60s Physical Burst

      Do 10 wall push-ups, 20 jumping jacks, or squeeze a stress ball hard to burn off adrenaline spikes.

      P - Paced Breathing

      4 In, 7 Out

      Breathe in through nose for 4 seconds, breathe out through mouth slowly for 7 seconds. Long exhales shut down fight-or-flight.

      P - Paired Relaxation

      Squeeze & Release

      Clench your fists and shoulders as tight as you can for 5 seconds... then drop them completely limp like a noodle for 10 seconds.
