How Peeking Works

The board stays visible to both players at all times! All pieces normally show concealed shield backs (🔵 Blue and 🔴 Red).

  • Peek an Individual Piece: Press and hold any of your own pieces on the board to sneak a peek at its rank!
  • Select & Move: Tap your piece to select it and see valid move dots. Tap a target square to move or attack.
  • Hold to Peek All: Press and hold the golden 👁️ Hold to Peek My Army button at the bottom to illuminate all of your pieces at once. Release to instantly conceal them again!

Classic US Ranks (1 is Highest, 9 is Lowest)

  • 1 — Marshal: Defeats 2–9 & Spy. Loses to Bombs and to the Spy (if Spy attacks first).
  • 2 — General: Defeats 3–9 & Spy.
  • 3 — Colonel: Defeats 4–9 & Spy.
  • 4 — Major: Defeats 5–9 & Spy.
  • 5 — Captain: Defeats 6–9 & Spy.
  • 6 — Lieutenant: Defeats 7–9 & Spy.
  • 7 — Sergeant: Defeats 8, 9 & Spy.
  • 8 — Miner: Defeats 9 & Spy. DEFUSES BOMBS!
  • 9 — Scout: Sprints any open distance in a straight line.
  • S — Spy: Defeats Marshal (1) ONLY if Spy attacks first. Loses to all pieces if attacked.
  • B — Bomb: Immobile. Destroys any attacker except a Miner.
  • F — Flag: Immobile. Capture to WIN!
