Rules & Mobile Tips

1. First Turn: Your first piece must cover your color's designated starting point.

2. Corner-to-Corner: Each new piece must touch at least one piece of your own color at a corner (diagonally).

3. No Edge Contact: Your pieces may NEVER touch another piece of your own color edge-to-edge. Edges of different colors can touch freely!

4. Scoring: When nobody has legal moves, unplaced squares count as -1 pt each. Place all 21 pieces for a +15 bonus (+20 if your final piece was the 1-square monomino!).

📲 Play Offline on Phone: In your mobile browser (Chrome/Safari), tap the browser menu (⋮ or Share) and select "Add to Home Screen". The game runs 100% offline without internet.
